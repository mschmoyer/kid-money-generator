SchmoBucks

Build printable front-and-back family play money. This version prints 6 large bills on a full 8.5 × 11 landscape page, with only internal grey cut lines. The bills themselves are drawn large in the HTML, and the backdrop/portrait art scales with them.

3. Denominations

This sheet prints exactly: three $1 bills, one $5 bill, one $10 bill, and one $20 bill.

4. Number placement

The denomination text is drawn into the corner frames on both front and back, positioned relative to the inset artwork area. The artwork inset is intentionally small so the printed bills feel larger.

5. Duplex options

Recommended print setting: two-sided / duplex, Actual Size / 100%, and usually flip on short edge for landscape pages. The back page is mirrored left/right by default so denomination-specific backs match the correct fronts.
